"a maze with lava traps and a locked door"

A lightweight JS/Canvas port of the maze scene generated by harness/generation/maze.py (seed 0), playable directly in this tab. The Python/pymunk engine is the verified ground truth for this scene; this is a demo of it, not a replacement. Click the corridor view below, then use the arrow keys.

▶

Loads the picked scene, runs the honest policy (visits what the objective actually requires, in order, never forces a solid obstacle), then resets and runs the adversarial policy (beelines for the goal, rams anything in the way, only backs off to fetch a requirement if ramming genuinely doesn't work), and reports whether the adversary found a shortcut the honest agent didn't take. Same real-time physics as free play below, just driven by a scripted policy instead of your keyboard.

Simplified from the real engine, on purpose: jump and horizontal movement are independent axes here (the real engine's single discrete action per tick is what made navigator.py's jump-composition bug possible, and this port sidesteps that class of bug rather than reproducing it). Collision is axis-separated AABB, not pymunk's full 2D polygon resolution. One hardcoded scene, no ramps (sloped collision isn't reimplemented here). Everything visible, the door unlocking, the hazard, the goal check, runs on the same event rules as the real objective predicate language.
